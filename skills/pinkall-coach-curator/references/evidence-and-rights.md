# Evidence and rights policy

## Evidence grade

Grade the method, not the prestige of the author or institution.

| Grade | Minimum meaning |
|---|---|
| E4 | Systematic review, meta-analysis, or robust replicated result |
| E3 | Controlled experiment or credible field experiment |
| E2 | Peer-reviewed empirical, longitudinal, or strong observational study |
| E1 | Transparent large proprietary dataset or observational vendor analysis |
| E0 | Practitioner opinion, book, course, case study, or anecdote |

Do not upgrade an E0 claim because several practitioners repeat it unless the records
are genuinely independent and the underlying method supports a higher grade.

## Provenance

Use one of: `academic`, `institutional`, `practitioner`, `vendor_data`,
`pinkall_internal`.

Record conflicts of interest, sample, method, date, jurisdiction, retraction status,
and important limitations when known.

## Rights status

| Status | Runtime rule |
|---|---|
| green | Commercial reuse or the intended transformation is explicitly licensed |
| amber | Metadata and a curator-authored claim only; legal review required before retaining extracts or embeddings |
| red | Do not ingest or derive a distributable/runtime asset without permission |

`Public`, `free`, `purchased`, and `available online` are not rights statuses.

Keep a locator such as DOI, URL, page, chapter, section, or timestamp. Keep excerpts
minimal and only when the rights record permits them. Prefer curator-authored factual
paraphrases and bibliographic metadata.

## Contradictions

Create an explicit relation when two claims disagree. Record whether the conflict may
come from population, geography, call stage, measurement, time, or methodology. A
contradiction lowers confidence until a narrower applicability rule resolves it.
