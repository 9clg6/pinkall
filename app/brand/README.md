# Pinkall identity — The Shift

The Pinkall mark is built from two offset carbon forms separated by one precise
pink incision. It represents the small, deliberate shift that turns a raw
conversation into a better next action.

It is intentionally category-independent: the mark must never be redrawn as a
phone, microphone, waveform, speech bubble, headset, spark, or literal `P`.

## Master assets

- `pinkall-mark.svg` — transparent vector symbol.
- `pinkall-lockup.svg` — horizontal symbol and lowercase wordmark.
- `pinkall-app-icon.svg` — opaque master for iOS and Android.
- `pinkall-app-icon-macos.svg` — macOS squircle master.

## Core colors

- Carbon: `#171519`
- Porcelain: `#F6F5F3`
- Raised porcelain: `#FBFAF8`
- Pink: `#D31E7D`

The logo itself never uses a gradient. Lilac belongs to ambient product motion,
not to the master identity.

## Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the width of the pink incision around the symbol.
Use the full-color mark from 20 px upward. Below 20 px, use the two carbon forms
with the incision left as negative space.
